Meet Meaghan Janedis

Meaghan is a seasoned startup CEO with a long history of helping people achieve their goals. She began her career on Wall Street, building relationships with her firms' key clients, then followed her entrepreneurial spirit to found companies across industries close to her passions — parenthood, athletics, eldercare — with ethics at the core of everything she does.

Eagle Point Coaching brings all of that experience to a more personal level, helping people achieve their professional and personal aspirations.

  • Captained her college tennis team to the 1992 & 1993 NCAA Div. III Championship
  • Licensed in Series 6, 7, 63 & SIE — Bear, Stearns, Merrill Lynch & New York Life
  • Founder & CEO of Eagle Point Coaching and multiple companies before it
  • Personally mentors young adults launching their educational and professional careers
