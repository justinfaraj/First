About

Meet Meaghan Janedis

Meaghan spent over a decade in senior leadership before turning to coaching full-time. She works with founders, executives, and rising leaders who have built successful careers but feel boxed in by them — helping them define what freedom actually looks like and build a path there without walking away from what they've already earned.

Her approach blends mindset work with practical business and career strategy, drawn from real boardroom experience rather than theory. Sessions are direct, honest, and built around measurable change.
